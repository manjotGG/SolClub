Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my wallet?expand_more
Click the "Connect Wallet" button in the top navigation. You can either create a managed wallet (we generate a keypair for you) or link an existing Phantom/Solflare wallet address. Managed wallets give you a private key you must save — it's shown only once.
How does cashback work?expand_more
Every time you make a purchase through a SolClub partner merchant, you automatically earn cashback in SOL. The percentage depends on your loyalty tier — higher tiers earn more. Cashback is calculated instantly and reflected in your dashboard.
What are Mystery NFTs?expand_more
After qualifying transactions, you may receive a Mystery NFT — a digital collectible minted on the Solana blockchain. These can be common, rare, or legendary. They're automatically minted to your connected wallet address.
How do I make a payment?expand_more
Tap the QR scanner button (bottom right) or go to Pay in the sidebar. Scan a merchant's QR code or manually enter their wallet address and the SOL amount. The payment is recorded on-chain and you earn loyalty rewards automatically.
Is my private key safe?expand_more
Managed wallet private keys are encrypted at rest using Fernet symmetric encryption. The key is displayed only once during creation. We never store plaintext keys. For maximum security, import your key into Phantom Wallet and use it from there.
