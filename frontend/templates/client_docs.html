What is SolClub?

SolClub is a Solana-powered loyalty and rewards platform that connects merchants with customers through on-chain transactions, cashback rewards, and digital collectibles.

rocket_launch

How It Works

1. Sign up & connect. Create an account via Google OAuth or manual registration. Then either generate a managed Solana wallet or link your existing Phantom/Solflare wallet.

2. Make purchases. Pay partner merchants using SOL. Scan their QR code or enter their wallet address from the Pay screen.

3. Earn automatically. Every transaction is tracked on-chain. You earn cashback (SOL), loyalty XP, tier upgrades, and Mystery NFTs — all automatically.

4. Level up. The more you transact, the higher your loyalty tier climbs. Higher tiers unlock bigger cashback percentages and rarer NFT drops.

Payment Flow

For Customers: Tap the QR scanner FAB → scan merchant's QR or paste their wallet address → enter amount → confirm. Your transaction is recorded and rewards are calculated instantly.

For Merchants: Go to Receive → enter the amount you want → generate a dynamic QR. Or use the "My QR" button for a static wallet QR that accepts any amount. Customers scan and pay directly.

Security

  • ✓ Session tokens signed with HMAC-SHA256
  • ✓ All API routes require authenticated sessions
  • ✓ Google OAuth 2.0 for social login
  • ✓ Private key shown exactly once — never stored in plaintext
